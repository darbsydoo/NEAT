# Naked Eye Astronomy Training

A sky map for practising the constellations at home, so you recognise them when you look up. The whole sky is one continuous field of real stars that you can pan, zoom and turn. Star training shows you a patch of it the way it would look from your latitude, with no lines or names. Find the constellation, reveal it, then learn its notable stars and its neighbours.

A magnitude slider sets how many stars you see, from a city sky to a dark rural one, so you can practise under the conditions you actually have.

Special stars adds short guided tours: finding your way from the Big Dipper and Orion, the nearest bright stars, the life of a star, and what a star's colour tells you.

It's a learning tool, not a stargazing app. For finding stars under the night sky, an app like Star Walk 2 is better.

Open it: https://darbsydoo.github.io/NEAT/

On iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. It then runs as an app and works offline.
