# NEAT
Naked Eye Astronomy Training 

A flashcard deck for practising the constellations at home, so you recognise them when you look up. Each card hides a constellation among real background stars. Find it, reveal it, then learn its star names, the physics of its notable stars, and its neighbours.

It's a learning tool, not a stargazing app. For finding stars under the night sky, an app like SkyView is better.

**Open the deck:** https://darbsydoo.github.io/NEAT/

On iPhone, open it in Safari and choose Share → Add to Home Screen. It then runs as an app and works offline.
